ALGORITHM NOTES

What is happening under the hood?

BFS · Queue

Visits cells in increasing distance from the start. In an unweighted grid, the first route found is a shortest route.

DFS · Stack

Follows one available direction as far as possible, then backtracks when it reaches a dead end. Its route may be longer.

Complexity

Both algorithms take O(V + E) time and O(V) extra space, where V is the number of open cells and E is the number of valid neighbour connections.
